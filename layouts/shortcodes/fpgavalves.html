Set the handles, follow the gas

The amber OFF handle points at the blocked port. Go by the named tubes and parts. The layout won't match your case.

Ambient check

Pump ON. V1–V6 normal, exhaust to free air.

Check: Leave the well disconnected until both readings settle.

All valve settings · print reference

Normal: V1 trap top → gauge · V2 gauge → pump · V3 pump → V4 · V4 → CO₂ · V5 O₂ → check valve · V6 → free air. Diverting closes the other branch. Sealing V6 closes both outlets.

Starting OFF-handle directions: V1 toward the V3 backflush line. V2 toward its luer. V3 toward the V1 backflush line. V4 toward its collection luer. V6 toward the white return. Leave V5 in its marked starting position. During backflush, point V1 and V2 toward the gauge and V3 toward V4. For injection, point V2 toward the gauge. For collection, point V4 toward CO₂. To use the white return, point V6 toward free air. To seal, stop the pump, then point V6 toward the check valve.
