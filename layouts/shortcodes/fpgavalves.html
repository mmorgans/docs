Set the valves by operating phase

Choose the step you are about to perform. The guide shows the gas path, pump state, and position of all six valves.

Position means flow path, not handle direction. Handles and plumbing can differ between builds. Confirm which physical direction produces each named state on your unit. A valve marked “outside active path” does not control the selected flow.
